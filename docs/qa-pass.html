Boot

This must come first, because the wrong ELF looks almost identical and every check below would be measuring the old build.

  1. 01 · blocker
    Set the card to channel 5 before inserting it.
    BootCard.ini calls channel 5 FMCB 1.953. It is the only channel whose config references mmce?: paths, which is how it reaches the SD card at all.
  2. 02 · blocker
    Power on and hold L1 until OPL appears.
    L1 is FMCB's LK_L1_E1, bound to mmce0:/APPS/OPNPS2LD.ELF — the file just deployed. Without L1 the auto chain runs instead and loads the older OPL baked inside the card image.
  3. 03 · blocker
    Open Settings and confirm a SHELF UI row exists.
    This is the only way to tell the builds apart — both report the same version string, because they are the same upstream commit. No SHELF UI row means stop and re-boot holding L1.
01

Four patterns that have never rendered on a TV

The card previously held only BG, COV, COVHD and LGO. PANEL, BTN, SCR and SCR2 have existed solely in the previewer until now. Press □ SQUARE on a title to reach the details page.

  1. 04 · PANEL
    Check the field behind the logo flips polarity per game.
    A pale logo should sit on a dark field and a dark logo on a pale one. Metal Gear Solid 3 is the title that forced this feature — check it specifically.
  2. 05 · PANEL
    Check the fade ramp at the background's right edge.
    The background should dissolve into the panel without a visible seam or step. The ramp is a 96×4 source stretched to 267×180, so any banding shows here first.
  3. 06 · BTN
    Find the play button between the description and the screenshots.
    Icon and label both left-aligned inside the button, not centred. It is decorative — it does not respond to input.
  4. 07 · SCR / SCR2
    Confirm two screenshots appear below the button.
    Two visibly different frames, not the same image twice. They are placeholders cut from each game's own key art — judge the geometry, not the content.
  5. 08 · spacing
    Measure the gap above and below the play button by eye.
    Even. This is the spacing that regressed four times and was only ever judged against empty screenshot boxes — this is its first honest test.
  6. 09 · LGO
    Look closely at the logo's edges.
    Smooth. Logos were deliberately left truecolour because palettising them destroyed the antialiased ring. Stair-stepped edges mean that decision was wrong and the logos need re-deriving.
02

256-colour art

Backgrounds, covers and large covers are newly quantised. Measured error was low and no banding was visible on a monitor — the TV is the real test. Undithered won the comparison, so any banding you find is the case that reopens it.

  1. 10 · backgrounds
    Look for banding in smooth dark areas — smoke, sky, glow.
    Continuous tone. Banding appears as concentric steps in a gradient. Flat cel-shaded regions are safe; the risk is anywhere the art blends.
  2. 11 · worst case
    Check Naruto (SLPS_255.89) specifically.
    It had the highest quantisation error in the batch. If anything bands, it bands here first.
  3. 12 · grid covers
    Scan the whole list for colour shifts in the cover tiles.
    Covers look as they did before. Twelve are resident at once, so this is also the densest palette load in the build.
  4. 13 · COVHD
    Compare the large cover on the details page against its grid tile.
    The large one is where quantisation shows most — 300×450 against 100×150. Gradients on box art are the thing to watch.
03

The failure signal

New in this build: a texture that fails to load no longer fails silently.

Magenta checkerboard means a texture failed

If any art slot shows a magenta-and-purple checker instead of an image, that asset was rejected or missing. Before this build the slot simply drew nothing, which was indistinguishable from art that had never been made. Note the game and which slot — that pair identifies the file exactly.

  1. 14 · blocker
    Page through every title watching for magenta.
    None. All 224 art files were verified as the right format before deploying, so a checker here means something the checks could not see.
04

SHELF panel

Off by default, so nothing below happens until you turn it on. The pages it routes to are deliberately empty — what is under test is the panel, the routing, and whether you can always get back out.

  1. 15 · toggle
    Settings → set SHELF UI to On.
    It was Off when you arrived. That default is deliberate and is the campaign's revert path.
  2. 16 · trigger
    At the list's left edge, hold LEFT. Then try L3.
    Both open the panel. A tap of LEFT at the edge must still wrap or page exactly as it always has.
  3. 17 · animation
    Watch the slide, then cancel it mid-open.
    Smooth over roughly a fifth of a second, and cancellable before it finishes — that was allowed on purpose.
  4. 18 · routing
    Enter Home, Library and Apps, leaving each with ○ CIRCLE.
    Each shows a "fills this in" placeholder and each lets you back out. A stub you cannot leave is a hang from where you are sitting.
  5. 19 · routing
    Select Settings from the panel.
    The ordinary settings screen, unmodified. The panel should close before the screen transition rather than sitting on top of it.
  6. 20 · footer
    Read the panel's bottom line.
    Online or Offline, matching reality. Free space is deliberately absent — no query exists for this device class, and a made-up number would be worse than none.
  7. 21 · revert
    Set SHELF UI back to Off and retry both triggers.
    Completely inert — no panel, no flicker, stock behaviour. This is the property the whole campaign rests on.
05

Stress

The details page now costs about a third of the texture pool where it used to cost three quarters. This is where that should show.

  1. 22 · churn
    Scroll the list hard, then bounce list → details → back ten times on different games.
    No hang, no flicker, no art arriving late or drawing over the wrong game. Eviction churn was the cost of the old truecolour batch — it should be noticeably calmer.

What to send back

  • The check number — 01 to 22.
  • Game and slot for anything visual: "MGS3, the panel behind the logo".
  • A photo for banding or edge quality. Descriptions of banding are unreliable; photographs are not.
  • Anything magenta, immediately — that is a file problem, not a layout one.
If a build misbehaves: power-cycle without holding L1. The auto chain loads the older OPL from inside the card image, which is untouched by any of this work. Nothing you can do from the checklist above can take that away.
